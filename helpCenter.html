Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order? ⌄
Go to Account → My Orders, or tap the 🛒 icon on Home after checking out. You'll see a live status timeline from "Order confirmed" to "Delivered".
How do I change my delivery address? ⌄
Go to Account → Delivery Address to add or update where your orders get delivered. It's also editable right from the Checkout page.
What payment methods are accepted? ⌄
Cash on Delivery, GCash, and Credit/Debit Card. You can save your preferred methods under Account → Payment Method.
Can I cancel or edit an order after placing it? ⌄
Not yet from the app directly — please reach out through Feedback or the contact options below and we'll help you sort it out.
How do I save items to Favorites? ⌄
Tap the heart ♡ icon on any product card, on Home or Categories. It'll turn into ♥ and show up under the Favorites tab.
